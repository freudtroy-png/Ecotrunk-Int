About ETL

Engineering a Greener Tomorrow

Ecotrunk International Ltd (ETL) is a firm of Technologists, Consultants, and Managers incorporated in the Republic of Kenya as a firm of Experts. The firm has broad experience in Project Development & Management, Engineering Systems Development and Consultancy in the renewable energy industry.

Since its establishment in 2014, Ecotrunk has been deeply dedicated to enhancing the global environment while promoting local development. Our team brings together qualified engineers, environmental specialists, and project managers with decades of combined experience in Kenya's energy sector.

Our headquarters are located at the Professional Centre along Ngong Road in Nairobi, from where we coordinate projects across multiple counties.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

2014

Incorporation

Ecotrunk International Ltd is incorporated in the Republic of Kenya as a firm of Experts in renewable energy technology and management.

2016

First Major Project

Delivered our first utility-scale solar feasibility study, establishing our reputation for technical rigour and reliable project management.

2018

Portfolio Expansion

Expanded into wind and hydropower consultancy, building a multi-technology portfolio serving clients across East Africa.

2020

Covid-19 Response

Adapted operations to support essential energy infrastructure during the pandemic, ensuring continuity of critical power projects.

2022

50th Project Milestone

Reached 50 completed projects across 7 Kenyan counties, solidifying our position as a trusted partner in renewable energy development.

2024 & Beyond

Regional Expansion

Extending our reach into neighbouring East African markets while deepening our impact in Kenya's transition to 100% clean energy by 2030.
